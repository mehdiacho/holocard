holocard

Your GitHub, as a collectible card.

Your top language sets the type, followers set the HP, your best repos become attacks, and stars decide how rare you are. Drop it in your README with one line.

![holocard](…/api/card?user=you)
TypeTop language, weighted by stars
HPFollowers, 40 to 340
AttacksYour two most-starred repos
RarityTotal stars, Common to Legendary
StageHow long you've been on GitHub
Holo foilRare cards and up shimmer
Cards are cached for a few hours. Add &animate=0 for a still card. Not affiliated with GitHub.
